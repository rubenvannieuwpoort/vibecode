How to use:

  • Upload an image using the "Choose Image" button
  • Enter a complex transformation in the textbox (use z as the variable)
  • Click "Apply Transform" to see the result
  • Try the preset transformations for quick examples

Supported functions:

  • Basic operations: +, -, *, /, ^
  • Functions: sqrt(), exp(), log(), sin(), cos(), tan()
  • Complex functions: conj(), abs(), arg(), re(), im()
  • Constants: i, pi, e
